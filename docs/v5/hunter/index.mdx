# Hunter: the Reckoning 5e

:::tip[In short]
You play an ordinary person who has seen a monster and decided to do something about it. Pick your path below: never played a tabletop RPG, already know how they work, or running the game.
:::

Something happened to your character. Maybe a neighbour stopped aging, maybe a patient's wounds closed in front of them, maybe a friend walked into the woods and came back wrong. Most people look away. Your hunter did not.

In a session, you and a few friends play a **cell** — a small group of hunters with jobs, families, and no superpowers to speak of. One person, the Storyteller, plays the world and everything hiding in it. You investigate, prepare, and strike, and you pay for every mistake with injuries, suspicion, and fraying nerves.

What makes Hunter different is **desperation**. Hunters are outmatched. The rules let you push harder than is safe, and the price of pushing is that the monsters notice you, or that your own reasons for hunting turn against you.
