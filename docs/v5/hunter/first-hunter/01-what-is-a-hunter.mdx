# What Is a Hunter?

_Step 1 of 9_

:::tip[In short]
A hunter is an ordinary person who learned that monsters are real and chose to fight back — with no special powers, just stubbornness, friends, and whatever tools they can find.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Every decision in the following steps answers one question: _what kind of ordinary person does this?_ Knowing the premise makes the rest of the sheet feel like describing a person instead of filling in a form.

## The world

The game is set in our world, today. Vampires, shapeshifters, ghosts, and stranger things exist, but they hide well and most people never notice. Police, doctors, and journalists explain the odd cases away.

## The moment everything changed

Your hunter had a moment when explaining it away stopped working. The game calls it **the Reckoning**. It may have been one terrible night or a slow pile of evidence. Either way, your hunter now knows, and cannot pretend otherwise.

## The cell

Hunters rarely survive alone. They form a **cell**: a few people who share information, cover for each other, and plan hunts. Your cell is the other players' characters. Some things on your sheet — Desperation and Danger — belong to the whole cell rather than to you.

## What a session feels like

Most of a session is investigation and preparation: asking questions, following leads, getting the right gear, keeping your normal life from falling apart. Fights happen, but they are dangerous and short. A good hunt is one you planned well enough to survive.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Do hunters have powers?</summary>

Mostly no. A few hunters have strange gifts, called Endowments, but they are small next to what the monsters can do. Your edge is preparation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it a horror game?</summary>

Yes. Your hunter can get badly hurt, lose people, or lose themselves. Talk with your group about what topics you want to keep out of the story.

</details>
