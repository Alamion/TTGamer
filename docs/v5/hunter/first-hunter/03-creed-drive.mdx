# Creed and Drive

_Step 3 of 9_

:::tip[In short]
Your **Creed** is _how_ your hunter fights. Your **Drive** is _why_ they cannot stop. Your **Redemption** is what pulls them back when the Drive turns sour.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

The Creed tells the group what your hunter brings to the cell — a researcher, a fighter, a fixer. The Drive decides when your hunter will take risks, and it is at the heart of Despair (step 9): when a hunt goes badly, the Drive is what cracks.

## Decide

- When your cell has a problem, do you want to be the one who builds the solution, prays over it, researches it, shoots it, or knows someone who can help?
- Think back to step 1. Is your hunter chasing answers, revenge, a promise, profit, pride, envy, or a chance to make up for something?
- If that motivation ever broke your hunter, what would help them recover? Write that as **Redemption**.

:::note[Example: Lena]

- **Creed:** Faithful — she carries a rosary and believes the cold thing can be driven off.
- **Drive:** Atonement — she hunts because she failed that patient.
- **Redemption:** She keeps a list of people she has pulled back from the edge; reading it reminds her why she keeps going.
  :::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Faithful sounds religious. Is it?</summary>

It can be, but it does not have to be. It covers any hunter who fights with conviction and ritual.
