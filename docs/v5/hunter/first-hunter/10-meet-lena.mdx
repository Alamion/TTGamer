# Meet Lena

:::tip[In short]
Here is the hunter we built along the way. Compare her with yours, then join your cell.
:::

## Why Lena looks like this

- Her **Concept**, **Ambition**, and **Desire** came first ([step 2](./02-concept-ambition-desire.mdx)); everything else follows from a paramedic chasing one mistake.
- **Faithful** and **Atonement** ([step 3](./03-creed-drive.mdx)) explain why she hunts with prayer and first aid.
- **Composure 4** and **Manipulation 1** ([step 4](./04-attributes.mdx)) make her calm but honest to a fault.
- A **Balanced** skill spread ([step 5](./05-skills-specialties.mdx)) mixes her job and her new obsession.
- **Sense the Unnatural** ([step 6](./06-edges-perks.mdx)) is her reason to be in the cell.
- Her **Advantages**, **Flaws** ([step 7](./07-advantages-flaws.mdx)), and **Touchstones** ([step 8](./08-touchstones.mdx)) tie her to people who can help — or get hurt.
