# Finishing Touches

_Step 9 of 9_

:::tip[In short]
Health and Willpower size themselves from your Attributes. Agree on the cell's starting **Desperation** and **Danger** with your group, then add equipment and any notes.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

These are the boxes you will touch most during play. Health and Willpower show how much more your hunter can take; Desperation, Danger, and Despair show how close the cell is to breaking.

## Decide

- Check **Health** (Stamina + 3) and **Willpower** (Composure + Resolve). If an Advantage adds boxes, press **+** after the track.
- Ask the Storyteller for the cell's starting **Desperation** and **Danger** and write the same numbers on every hunter's sheet.
- Leave **Despair** unchecked — nobody starts in Despair.
- What does your hunter always carry? Add weapons under **Weapons** (damage, range, ammo) and everything else under **Inventory**. Types and gear are listed in [Weapons, Armor, and Gear](../reference/weapons-gear.mdx).

:::note[Example: Lena]
Stamina 3 gives Lena **6 Health**; Composure 4 + Resolve 2 give **6 Willpower**. Her cell starts with Desperation 1 and Danger 1. She carries trauma shears and a few road flares as weapons, plus a trauma kit, a pocket torch, a silver rosary, and spare keys to the ambulance.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>What are Desperation, Danger, and Despair, really?</summary>

See [Desperation, Danger, and Despair](../reference/desperation-danger-despair.mdx). You only need the short version now: Desperation adds dice when the hunt matters, Danger rises when the monsters notice you, and Despair is what happens when your Drive breaks.

</details>

:::info[Checkpoint]

- Health and Willpower show the right number of boxes.
- Desperation and Danger match the rest of your cell.
  :::
