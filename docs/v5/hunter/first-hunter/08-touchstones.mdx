# Touchstones and Convictions

_Step 8 of 9_

:::tip[In short]
Name one or two **Touchstones** — real people in your hunter's life — and give each a **Conviction**, a rule your hunter lives by because of them.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Touchstones remind everyone what the hunt is for. When your hunter is about to cross a line, their Convictions are what should make them hesitate. They also give the Storyteller someone to put in danger — use that to make the story personal.

## Decide

- Who in your hunter's normal life would they protect at any cost?
- What rule do they follow because that person exists?
- Does your cell have shared **Chronicle Tenets** — lines the group agreed nobody crosses? Write them in too.

:::note[Example: Lena]

- **Mark, her younger brother** — _Never leave anyone behind._
- **Tomas, her partner** — _No one dies on my watch if I can stop it._
- The cell's tenets: _Protect the living. Tell the truth to the cell. Leave no evidence for the police._
  :::

:::info[Checkpoint]

- At least one Touchstone with a Conviction.
