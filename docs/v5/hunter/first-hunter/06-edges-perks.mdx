# Edges and Perks

_Step 6 of 9_

:::tip[In short]
Take **two Edges with one Perk**, or **one Edge with two Perks**. An Edge is something only your hunter brings to the cell; a Perk makes it better in one specific way.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Edges are the answer to "why does the cell need _you_?" They come in three kinds:

- **Assets** — things your hunter can get: weapons, vehicles, explosives, lore.
- **Aptitudes** — things your hunter can do: build gear, get into systems, fly drones, work with animals.
- **Endowments** — strange gifts: sensing, repelling, or resisting the unnatural, or owning an artifact.

## Decide

- What will your hunter usually be asked to handle when the cell plans a hunt?
- Do you prefer one strong trick (one Edge, two Perks) or two different ones (two Edges, one Perk)?
- Endowments are rare and a bit mysterious — check with your Storyteller before taking one.

## On your sheet

Add a row to **Edges**, start typing the Edge name, and pick it from the list. Then add a row to **Perks** for each Perk: pick it from the list and its Edge fills in by itself. You can rename either to fit your story.
