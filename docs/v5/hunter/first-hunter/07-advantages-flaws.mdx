# Advantages and Flaws

_Step 7 of 9_

:::tip[In short]
Spend **7 points** on Advantages (contacts, allies, money, useful quirks) and take **2 points** of Flaws (problems that complicate your hunter's life).
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Advantages are the people and resources your hunter can call on outside a dice roll. Flaws give the Storyteller hooks: a debt, an enemy, a bad habit. Both make your hunter part of the world instead of a list of numbers.

## Decide

- Who would your hunter call at 3 a.m.? That is a **Contact** or an **Ally**.
- What does their job give them access to?
- What makes their life harder — something that will cause trouble at the worst moment?

Browse every Merit, Background, and Flaw with its dots in [Advantages and Flaws](../reference/advantages-flaws.mdx). Anything not on the list, agree on with your Storyteller: 1 dot is small, 5 is huge.

## On your sheet

Add entries under **Advantages** and **Flaws** and set their dots. Start typing and the lists suggest book names.

:::note[Example: Lena]

- Advantages: **Contacts — night staff at St. Anne hospital** (3), **Ally — Tomas, her ambulance partner** (2), **Iron Stomach** (2). Total 7.
- Flaws: **Insomnia** (1), **Obligation — double shifts** (1). Total 2.
  :::

:::info[Checkpoint]

- Advantages add up to 7, Flaws to 2.
