# Advantages and Flaws

:::tip[In short]
**Merits** are personal knacks, **Backgrounds** are what the world gives you (people, money, a safe house), **Flaws** are problems worth points. A new hunter spends 7 points on Advantages and takes 2 points of Flaws.
:::

Use the filters to show only one kind or one trait. Select a row for its details. On the sheet, the **Advantages** and **Flaws** lists suggest these names as you type.

## Reading the dots

- **••** — a fixed price.
- **•–•••••** — you choose how many dots to buy; more dots, more effect.
- **• per dot** — a Safe House feature you can buy several times, each dot adding more of it.

Some entries depend on another: Zeroed and Cobbler need a two-dot Mask, and the larger Safe House features need a Safe House of two dots or more. The summaries are reminders, not full rules — ask your Storyteller how an Advantage plays out in your chronicle.
