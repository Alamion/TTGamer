# Reading the Sheet

:::tip[In short]
Top to bottom: who you are, how you are holding up, what you are made of and know, what makes you special, why you hunt, who and what you have, and what you have done. Groups you rarely touch (portrait, biography, chronicle tenets) fold away.
:::

| Section              | What it holds                                                             | Explained in                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Hunter               | Portrait; Name, Concept, Creed, Drive; biography (folded)                 | [Step 2](../first-hunter/02-concept-ambition-desire.mdx), [Step 3](../first-hunter/03-creed-drive.mdx)                         |
| Condition            | Health and Willpower boxes (click to mark), Despair, Desperation, Danger  | [Damage and Willpower](../../rules/damage-willpower.mdx), [Desperation, Danger, and Despair](./desperation-danger-despair.mdx) |
| Attributes           | Raw capacity, 1–5 dots                                                    | [Attributes and Skills](../../rules/attributes-skills.mdx)                                                                     |
| Skills               | Training, 0–5 dots, with Specialties written next to the Skill            | [Step 5](../first-hunter/05-skills-specialties.mdx)                                                                            |
| Edges and Perks      | What only your hunter brings to the cell, as two tables                   | [Edges and Perks](../first-hunter/06-edges-perks.mdx)                                                                          |
| Aims and Convictions | Ambition, Desire, Redemption, Creed Fields, Touchstones, Chronicle Tenets | [Step 2](../first-hunter/02-concept-ambition-desire.mdx), [Step 8](../first-hunter/08-touchstones.mdx)                         |
| Advantages and Flaws | People, resources, and complications                                      | [Advantages and Flaws](./advantages-flaws.mdx)                                                                                 |

:::info[Views]
The **Full sheet** view shows everything; the **Brief** view keeps only what you roll and track during play. Switch with the view selector above the sheet.
:::
