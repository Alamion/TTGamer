# Attributes and Skills

:::tip[In short]
**Attributes** (1–5 dots) describe your raw capacity; **Skills** (0–5 dots) describe training. A roll almost always adds one of each. **Specialties** are narrow areas where a Skill shines.
:::

## Attributes

Nine Attributes sit in three columns. Two dots is an ordinary adult, three is noticeably good, five is the best anyone around has ever seen.

|              | Power                                 | Finesse                         | Resistance                              |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Physical** | Strength — lifting, hitting, breaking | Dexterity — speed, balance, aim | Stamina — endurance, shrugging off harm |
| **Social**   | Charisma — being liked and followed   | Manipulation — steering people  | Composure — keeping your head           |
| **Mental**   | Intelligence — knowledge, reasoning   | Wits — reacting and noticing    | Resolve — focus and determination       |

Stamina sets your Health; Composure and Resolve set your Willpower (see [Damage and Willpower](./damage-willpower.mdx)).

## Skills

Skills also come in three columns, loosely matching the Attributes they pair with most often — but any Attribute can pair with any Skill if the story fits.

- **Physical:** Athletics, Brawl, Craft, Driving, Firearms, Larceny, Melee, Stealth, Survival.
- **Social:** Animal Ken, Etiquette, Insight, Intimidation, Leadership, Performance, Persuasion, Streetwise, Subterfuge.
- **Mental:** Academics, Awareness, Finance, Investigation, Medicine, Occult, Politics, Science, Technology.

A Skill at 1 means you know the basics; at 3 you are a professional; at 5 you are one of the best.

## Specialties

A Specialty is a narrow part of a Skill you are especially good at — _Medicine (Trauma)_ or _Firearms (Shotguns)_. When a roll falls inside a Specialty, add one die. A Skill can hold several Specialties. On the sheet, write them in the line next to the Skill, separated by commas.
