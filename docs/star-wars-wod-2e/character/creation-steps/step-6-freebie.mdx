## Step 6: Spend Freebie Points (15 Points)

You have **15 Freebie Points** to fine-tune your character. These can raise existing traits or purchase Merits (see below).

### Freebie Cost Table

| Trait                    | Cost per dot       | Notes                                                                |
| ------------------------ | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attribute**            | 5 points           | Raises one Attribute by 1 (max 5)                                    |
| **Ability**              | 2 points           | Raises one Ability (Talent/Skill/Knowledge) by 1 (max 3 at creation) |
| **Virtue**               | 2 points           | Raises Conscience, Passion, or Self-Control by 1 (max 5)             |
| **Force Skill**          | 3 points           | Requires the Force Sensitive Merit first                             |
| **Force Power**          | 2 points per power | Requires at least one dot in the relevant Force Skill                |
| **Background**           | 1 point            | Raises a background rating by 1                                      |
| **Merit**                | 1 point per point  | Purchase merits at their listed point cost                           |
| **Force Points**         | 2 points           | Raises maximum Force Points by 1                                     |
| **Willpower**            | 2 points           | Raises maximum Willpower by 1                                        |
| **Dark Side Resistance** | 1 point            | Raises Dark Side Resistance by 1                                     |

### Merits & Flaws

:::caution[No separate pool]
**There are no separate points for Merits & Flaws.** You buy Merits with your **Freebie Points**, and Flaws give you **more Freebie Points** to spend. This is not a separate resource — it's all part of your 15 Freebie Points.
:::

**Merits** are special advantages that set your character apart — natural talent, unique training, cybernetics, or fortunate circumstances. **Flaws** are the burdens they carry — enemies, obligations, physical drawbacks, or psychological weaknesses.

| Rule                      | Detail                                                      |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Merit cost**            | A `+3` Merit costs **3 Freebie Points**                     |
| **Flaw rebate**           | A `-3` Flaw grants **3 bonus Freebie Points**               |
| **Net limit at creation** | Merits minus Flaws cannot exceed **7 points**               |
| **Alien Physiology**      | Species-specific traits are marked as **Alien Physiology**  |
| **Force-related Merits**  | Required to access Force Skills (e.g., **Force Sensitive**) |

**How it works:**

- You buy Merits by spending Freebie Points equal to their listed value. A `+3` Merit costs 3 Freebie Points from your pool.
- You take Flaws to gain bonus Freebie Points. A `-3` Flaw gives you 3 extra Freebie Points to spend on other traits.
- Net limit example: +5 in Merits and -3 in Flaws = net +2 ✓. +10 in Merits and -2 in Flaws = net +8 ✗ (over the 7-point limit).

**Species Physiology:** Non-human species often include biological Merits and Flaws as part of their template. For example, a Wookiee may have **Natural Weaponry (Claws)** as a merit and **Short Temper** as a flaw. These are factored into the species' Freebie Point adjustment.

**Choosing Your Merits & Flaws:**

- **Combat-oriented:** Daredevil, Iron Will, Natural Weaponry
- **Social-oriented:** Allies, Fame, Ambidextrous
- **Force-oriented:** Force Sensitive, Strong in the Force, Natural Force Power
- **Burdens:** Enemy, Dark Side Susceptibility, Code of Honor, Phobia

See the full [Merits & Flaws](../merits-flaws.mdx) catalog for descriptions, costs, and prerequisites.

### Spending Strategy

**Example:** _Jax_ spends his 15 Freebie Points: raises Dexterity from 4 to 5 (5 points), raises Blaster from 3 to 4 using the Practiced Merit (2 points — see Practiced in Merits & Flaws), raises Resources from 2 to 3 (1 point), and takes the Merit **Daredevil** (+3, costs 3 points). That's 5 + 2 + 1 + 3 = 11 points. He takes the Flaw **Soft-Hearted** (-1, gains 1 point) and **Enemy: Crimson Dawn** (-3, gains 3 points) for 4 additional points. Total spent: 11 + 4 = 15.
