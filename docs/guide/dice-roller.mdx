# Dice roller \{#dice-roller}

:::tip[In short]
The dice button in the top bar opens the dice panel. Click dice buttons or type a notation such as `2d6+3`, then press **Roll** or `Enter`. On the [character sheet](/universal_sheet) a trait's dice icon fills in its pool. Every roll lands in the history, where you can roll it again.
:::

## The dice panel \{#panel}

The dice button in the top bar shows and hides the panel. On most pages it slides in from the right; on the character sheet it is a **Dice Roller** tab of the [layout](./documents#layout), which you can move, float, or dock like any other tab.

- **Dice tabs.** **Standard** has buttons for d2–d20, d100, and Fudge dice; **D&D** adds **ADV** and **DIS** (advantage turns `1d20` into `2d20kh1`, the other button undoes it); **WoD** has the World of Darkness pools (below). A left click on a die adds one, a right click removes one; clicking the open tab again hides the buttons.
- **Notation.** Type in the field and a check mark or a cross tells you at once whether it can be rolled; a cross comes with what is wrong and marks the place. `Enter` or **Roll** rolls; **Clear** empties the field. The star saves the notation to your favorites.
- **The pending roll.** While the field holds a notation, it also shows next to the dice button in the top bar: a left click there rolls it without opening the panel, a right click clears it.
- **Result.** Every roll shows a short message with its total, and with the verdict when the roll has a goal.

## The WoD tab \{#wod}

**Rules** switches between **Classic** (Storyteller dice: a pool of d10 against a difficulty) and **V5**.

- **Classic.** **Difficulty** (6 by default) is the number each die must reach; **Successes needed** is how many successes the action takes. The buttons add a d10, a d10 where ones cancel successes (botches), or a d6. With successes needed set, the roll says **Success** with its margin or **Failure** with what it lacked.
- **V5.** Every die succeeds on 6 or more. **Special dice** chooses the line: **Hunger** (Vampire) or **Desperation** (Hunter); the second d10 button adds such a die, drawn in its own colour. **Count pairs of 10s as criticals** adds two successes for every pair of 10s; **Report special dice outcomes** names messy criticals and bestial failures (Hunger) or a Desperation die showing 1. See [dice pools](../wod-v5/rules/dice-pools) for the rules.

## Dice notation \{#notation}

A roll is dice and numbers joined by `+ - * / % ^` and parentheses: `2d6+3`, `(1d8+2)*2`. Spaces do not matter. Division rounds down. There are no functions such as `floor` or `max()`.

Click an example to roll it right there and see every die.

| Dice                 | Try it                               | Meaning                                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `2d6`                | <RollExample notation="2d6" />       | two six-sided dice; `d20` is one die           |
| `d100`               | <RollExample notation="d100" />      | a percentile die                               |
| `4dF`                | <RollExample notation="4dF" />       | Fudge dice: each is −1, 0, or +1               |
| `d[1,3,5]`, `d[2-5]` | <RollExample notation="3d[1,3,5]" /> | a die with the faces you list (ranges allowed) |

Modifiers follow the dice. Write them in any order: they always apply in this one — min and max, explode, reroll, unique, keep or drop, successes and failures, criticals, sort.

| Modifier                   | Try it                                                   | What it does                                                             |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `min3`, `max5`             | <RollExample notation="4d6min3" />                       | a die below 3 counts as 3; above 5 counts as 5                           |
| `!`                        | <RollExample notation="4d6!" />                          | explode: a die on its highest face adds another die                      |
| `!!`                       | <RollExample notation="4d6!!" />                         | compounding explode: the extra rolls add into the same die               |
| `!p`                       | <RollExample notation="4d6!p" />                         | penetrating explode: each extra die counts one less                      |
| `r`, `r3`, `r<3`           | <RollExample notation={'4d6r\u003c3'} />                 | reroll ones; reroll 3 or less; reroll below 3 — as often as it comes up  |
| `ro`, `ro<3`               | <RollExample notation={'4d6ro\u003c3'} />                | reroll once                                                              |
| `u`, `uo`                  | <RollExample notation="4d6u" />                          | reroll duplicates (every time, or once)                                  |
| `kh3` (`k3`), `kl2`        | <RollExample notation="4d6kh3" />                        | keep the highest 3, keep the lowest 2                                    |
| `dl1` (`d1`), `dh1`        | <RollExample notation="4d6dl1" />                        | drop the lowest one, drop the highest one                                |
| `>=6` right after the dice | <RollExample notation={'6d10\u003e=6'} />                | count successes: the total is how many dice reached 6                    |
| `f=1`                      | <RollExample notation={'6d10\u003e=6f=1'} />             | each 1 takes one success away (with a success count)                     |
| `cs`, `cf`                 | <RollExample notation="4d6cs" />                         | mark critical successes (highest face) and failures (lowest face)        |
| `csb`, `cfb`               | <RollExample notation="4d6csb" />                        | the same, and each one adds or takes away 1 from the total               |
| `s`, `sd`                  | <RollExample notation="6d6s" />                          | sort the dice up or down                                                 |
| `x2=10`                    | <RollExample notation={'8d10\u003e=6x2=10'} />           | every pair of dice showing 10 adds two successes (needs a success count) |
| `:h`                       | <RollExample notation={'3d10:h\u003e=6+2d10\u003e=6'} /> | right after the dice: these are special dice (Hunger or Desperation)     |

Where a modifier takes a condition (a _compare point_), write `=`, `>`, `<`, `>=`, `<=`, or `<>` (not equal) and a number: `4d10!>=9`, `3d6r<=2`. Use `<>`, not `!=`, which reads as an explosion.

A few things to know:

- Only one keep or drop applies to a group of dice: highest kept, then lowest kept, then highest dropped, then lowest dropped.
- Modifiers after parentheses apply to each roll inside: `(3d10+1d10)>=6` counts successes in both. `(1d4+1)d6` is not "1d4+1 six-sided dice": write such rolls in two steps.
- Dice are written with a small `d`, modifiers in small letters; `D` and `d%` are not dice here.
- Limits: 200 dice in a roll, 100,000 sides, 1,000 listed faces, 500 characters, and 100 explosions or rerolls of a die.

## History and favorites \{#history}

Under the panel, **History** keeps your last 50 rolls on this device, **Favorites** the notations you starred, **Recent** the last ten notations you typed. Click a roll to see each die, the character and traits it came from, the verdict, and the special dice. The ↻ button puts a notation back in the field; a right click on it rolls it again at once. Each list has its own **Clear**.

## 3D dice \{#3d}

With 3D dice on, d2–d20, d100, and Fudge dice are thrown on the screen, and the faces they land on are the result. While they roll, ✓ takes the roll as it is and ✗ cancels it; a click on a die throws it again (the history then says it was rerolled by hand). A roll with more than 200 dice, or with dice that have no 3D shape (other sizes, or listed faces), is rolled instantly instead; so is everything when 3D is off or the 3D dice cannot load (you will be told once).

## Settings \{#settings}

The gear opens the settings: 3D dice on or off, the colours of dice, their text, and special dice, **Dice feel** (from heavy to lively), roll sounds and their volume, and **Time to react** (the 3D dice wait that many seconds before settling, unless you accept the roll). **Include roll context**, **character name**, and **character stats** decide what a roll carries into the history and into [shared rolls](./roll-sharing). Sharing rolls to Discord or Matrix is set up here too.

## Rolling from a sheet \{#sheet-rolls}

A trait with a pool has a small dice icon. A left click puts its pool into the dice field (adding to what is there) together with the character's name and the trait; roll it from the panel, with `Enter`, or with the pending roll in the top bar. A right click rolls it at once. Rolls from a V5 sheet follow V5 rules and its special dice, whatever tab the panel shows. Clearing the field forgets the character.

## Rolls in the documentation \{#inline-rolls}

Rules pages show rolls as a dice icon with an underlined notation: click it to roll right there, and again to roll anew. These rolls stay on the page: they are not added to the history or shared. Some examples roll fixed results on purpose, so the text can explain them.
