# Experience \{#experience}

**Experience points (XP)** record advancement earned during play and pay for improvements allowed by your game’s rules. **Total** is all XP awarded so far, **Spent** is the part already used, and **Available** is what remains: Total minus Spent. For example, 15 Total and 9 Spent leave 6 Available.

:::tip[In short]
Record awards in Total and purchases in Spent. Available shows the unspent balance; it is not a separate award or a rating of the character’s power.
:::

Agree an improvement with the Storyteller before recording its cost. Eligible traits, training requirements, and prices come from the relevant game line and your chronicle’s agreements: a mortal, hunter, and vampire do not all buy the same abilities at universal prices. Consult the rules for that character rather than using these three bookkeeping totals as an advancement table.

Keep a short note of what you bought and why it fits the story. Increasing an XP total alone does not choose or purchase an improvement for you.
