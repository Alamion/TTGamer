# Damage and Willpower

:::tip[In short]
You have two tracks of boxes: **Health** (Stamina + 3) for the body and **Willpower** (Composure + Resolve) for the mind. Harm is either **superficial** (it fades) or **aggravated** (it lingers). A full track means you are **impaired**.
:::

## Health \{#health}

**Health** is the track of physical harm a character can withstand. Its capacity is normally **Stamina + 3**, with any applicable adjustments; marks record damage already suffered, rather than reducing that capacity. A Storyteller character instead has an adjustable track length chosen for that character. Superficial and aggravated marks distinguish lighter harm from lasting injuries. See [Recovery](#recovery) for healing and the [tracker guide](../../guide/template-editor/elements.mdx#trackers) for using the boxes.

## Willpower \{#willpower}

**Willpower** is mental resilience: it absorbs mental and social harm and can be spent on certain actions. Its normal capacity is **Composure + Resolve**; the Storyteller sheet instead lets you set its length. Damage marks show strain already taken or points spent, not a lower capacity. Spending one point marks one superficial damage and lets you reroll up to three eligible dice; Hunger dice cannot be rerolled this way. Recovery depends on the game line and the character’s actions: see [Recovery](#recovery) and the [tracker guide](../../guide/template-editor/elements.mdx#trackers).

## Two tracks

- **Health** takes physical harm: blows, bullets, falls, fire.
- **Willpower** takes mental and social harm: terror, humiliation, pressure. You also mark it when you spend Willpower to reroll dice.

The number of boxes comes from your Attributes. Some advantages add boxes; use the **−/+** buttons after the track for that.

## Two kinds of harm

- **Superficial** harm is bruises, scrapes, and nerves. Most ordinary attacks deal superficial Health damage, and it heals quickly.
- **Aggravated** harm is serious: deep wounds, supernatural claws, trauma that changes you. It heals slowly.

Aggravated marks always fill boxes first, followed by superficial marks.

## When a track is full

If you take superficial harm with no empty boxes left, one of your superficial marks becomes aggravated instead. Once every box holds a mark, you are **impaired**: the Storyteller applies a penalty to your rolls until you recover. A track completely filled with aggravated harm means you are taken out — badly hurt, broken, or worse.

:::info[How the sheet helps]
Click a box to cycle it: superficial (╱), aggravated (×), empty. You place marks yourself, so keep aggravated marks first and upgrade a superficial mark when the track is full. If your Stamina drops, marks past the last box are kept and come back when the track grows again.
:::

## Recovery \{#recovery}

For living characters, superficial Health damage can heal with rest and aggravated wounds need longer recovery and care, as the game line specifies. Vampires instead mend with their Blood; use the [Vampire rules](../vampire/reference/hunger-rouse.mdx) and the healing effects of [Blood Potency](../vampire/reference/blood-potency.mdx). Superficial Willpower damage returns when you rest or act on what matters to your character; aggravated Willpower damage needs something that truly restores your sense of self. Your Storyteller sets the exact pace.
