# Weapons and Gear

:::tip[In short]
A weapon’s damage bonus increases a successful attack’s harm. Its type, range, and ammunition describe what it can do in the scene.
:::

## Weapons \{#weapons}

**Weapons** are equipment used to attack, with V5 combat values describing how they inflict harm. A weapon’s **type** identifies its category, while its **damage bonus** adds to the margin of a successful attack; it does not add that many dice to the attack pool. **Range** describes effective distance, and **ammunition**, when recorded, shows available shots. The Storyteller uses those details to judge whether and how the attack is possible.

For example, a winning margin of two with a +2 damage bonus produces four damage before the target’s applicable protections. The target’s nature and the attack determine how that harm is treated: the same weapon can affect a living person and an undead vampire differently. A recorded range or ammunition count does not automatically change a roll or spend shots.

For entering carried items and managing weapon cards, use the [equipment guide](../../guide/sheet.mdx#equipment). Use your game line’s rules and the Storyteller’s ruling for unusual weapons and circumstances.
