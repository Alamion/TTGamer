# Mortals \{#mortal}

A **mortal** is an ordinary living human character, without a hunter’s specialized traits or a vampire’s undead nature. The full Mortal sheet represents a V5 core character with individual Attributes, Skills, Health, Willpower, relationships, and possessions. It describes who that person is in detail; it does not make them a simplified opponent just because they are human.

:::tip[In short]
Use the full Mortal sheet when individual traits matter. Use a Storyteller character sheet when a few broad pools are enough to run someone the players meet.
:::

A mortal can be a player’s character or someone portrayed by the Storyteller. That role is separate from the choice of sheet: a recurring human investigator may need full traits, while a passing witness may only need a few [Storyteller pools](../storyteller/storyteller-characters.mdx#standard-pools).

For the core numbers, see [Attributes and Skills](./attributes-skills.mdx) and [Health and Willpower](./damage-willpower.mdx). Rules for supernatural characters belong to their own game line; the Mortal sheet does not grant those abilities or resources.
