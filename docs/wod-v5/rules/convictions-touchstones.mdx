# Touchstones and Convictions \{#touchstones-and-convictions}

A **Conviction** is a personal belief that guides a character's choices. A **Touchstone** is the person tied to that belief, someone whose place in the character's life makes the principle personal. Each pair connects what the character believes with someone who gives that belief meaning.

:::tip[In short]
A Conviction belongs to one character; a Touchstone is the person connected to it.
Chronicle Tenets are the principles the whole group agrees on for the continuing story.
:::

For a mortal character, _Never abandon someone who needs help_ could be a Conviction, with _Mara, the neighbor who took me in_ as its Touchstone. The paired columns identify the person and the associated belief. These are relationships and principles to draw on when portraying the character.

## Chronicle Tenets \{#chronicle-tenets}

**Chronicle Tenets** are the principles the players and Storyteller agree on for their **chronicle**, the continuing story told across game sessions. They give the group a shared moral frame, while a Conviction describes one character's personal belief.

The field holds the agreed principles, for example _Protect people caught in the crossfire_ and _Do not betray someone who asks for shelter_. These shared descriptions do not assign mortal characters any line-specific resources or consequences; use the relevant game's rules for mechanical effects.
