# For the Storyteller

:::tip[In short]
The players have full character sheets; the people and things they meet need much less. These pages show how to keep them as short sheets that roll their own dice, and how to tie them together into groups.
:::

Both kinds belong to the V5 rules, so they work the same in a Hunter chronicle, a Vampire chronicle, and your own settings on V5. In **New** they sit under the V5 rules, next to the mortal.
