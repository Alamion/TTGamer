# Vampire: the Masquerade 5e

:::tip[In short]
You play a vampire: a person who died, rose again, and now has to drink blood to go on. Pick your path below: never played a tabletop RPG, already know how they work, or running the game.
:::

Someone chose your character, killed them, and gave them their blood. Your character woke up hungry. They still have a name, a flat, maybe a job and people who miss them, but they now live at night, hide what they are, and feed on the living.

In a session, you and a few friends play a **coterie** — a small group of vampires who need each other to survive the politics of the undead and the dangers of the night. One person, the Storyteller, plays the city and everyone in it. You scheme, hunt, and bargain, and the price of every success is paid in blood.

What makes Vampire different is **Hunger**. The more your vampire uses their powers, the hungrier they get, and the hungrier they are, the more the **Beast** inside takes over their dice. The game is about staying human while being a monster: your **Humanity** wears down each time the Beast wins.
