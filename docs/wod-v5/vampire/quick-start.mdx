# Quickstart

:::tip[In short]
Roll **Attribute + Skill** in ten-sided dice, count **6+**, pairs of 10s count four. As many of those dice as your **Hunger** become **Hunger dice**: a 10 on one can make a win messy, a 1 on one can make a failure bestial. Using your Blood calls for a **Rouse check**, which may raise Hunger.
:::

## What you need

- A vampire sheet open in the [Sheet Manager](/universal_sheet) (a second tab works well).
- A handful of ten-sided dice, or the site's dice roller.
- Two to five players and a Storyteller.

## The one roll you need to know

1. The Storyteller names an **Attribute** and a **Skill**. Add their dots: that is your pool.
2. Swap as many of those dice as your **Hunger** for Hunger dice (a different colour). With Hunger 2 and a pool of seven, you roll five normal dice and two Hunger dice.
3. Each die showing **6 or more** is a success; **each pair of 10s** is worth four.
4. Reach the **Difficulty** (usually 2–4 successes) and you succeed.
5. Once per roll, spend a point of **Willpower** to reroll up to three failed _normal_ dice. Hunger dice are never rerolled.

:::note[Example: Mirela]
Mirela talks her way past a doorman: **Charisma 3 + Persuasion 3**, plus a die for her _Seduction_ specialty. Her Hunger is 2, so two of the seven dice are Hunger dice. The Difficulty is 3.

Rolling from the sheet does the swap for you: click a trait and the dice panel turns as many dice as your Hunger into Hunger dice. Details: [Dice Pools](../rules/dice-pools.mdx) and [Hunger and Rouse checks](./reference/hunger-rouse.mdx).

## When the Beast rolls

- **Messy critical:** you win with a pair of 10s and at least one of them is on a Hunger die. You get what you wanted, but like an animal would — someone sees, something breaks, or your conscience takes a Stain.
- **Bestial failure:** you fail and at least one Hunger die shows a 1. The Beast shows itself; usually you have to act out a **Compulsion** of your clan for the rest of the scene.

## Using your Blood

- Waking each night, healing, a burst of strength or speed, and most Discipline powers call for a **Rouse check**: roll one die. On a 6 or more nothing happens; otherwise your Hunger rises by one.
- At Hunger 5 you cannot rouse your Blood on purpose. Feed first.
- Feeding lowers Hunger. Without killing, a young vampire can only bring it down to 1.
- On the sheet, the **Rouse check** button rolls the die and raises Hunger on a failure; each Discipline power that costs Blood has its own button, which rolls as many checks as the power costs.

## Humanity

- **Humanity** (starting at 7) is how much of a person your vampire still is.
- Doing something monstrous marks **Stains** on the Humanity track, from its far end.
- At the end of the session, if you have Stains, press **Remorse check**: on a success the Stains fade; on a failure you lose a dot of Humanity, and the Stains fade anyway.

Details: [Humanity and Stains](./reference/humanity-stains.mdx).

## Make a vampire in ten minutes

| Step | What to do                                                                  | Detail                                                          |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Name, concept, sire, an **Ambition** (long-term) and a **Desire** (tonight) | [Concept](./first-vampire/02-concept-sire.mdx)                  |
| 2    | Pick a **clan**; note its Bane and Compulsion                               | [Clan](./first-vampire/03-clan.mdx)                             |
| 3    | Attributes: one at 4, three at 3, four at 2, one at 1                       | [Attributes](./first-vampire/04-attributes.mdx)                 |
| 4    | Skills: pick a spread (below), plus one free Specialty                      | [Skills](./first-vampire/05-skills-specialties.mdx)             |
| 5    | Two clan **Disciplines** at 2 and 1, one power per dot                      | [Disciplines](./first-vampire/06-disciplines.mdx)               |
| 6    | A **predator type**: a Specialty, a Discipline dot, Advantages and Flaws    | [Predator type](./first-vampire/07-predator-type.mdx)           |

| 8    | One to three **Convictions**, each with a **Touchstone**; Humanity 7        | [Convictions](./first-vampire/09-convictions-touchstones.mdx)   |
| 9    | Generation and Blood Potency, Hunger 1, Health and Willpower                | [Blood and finishing](./first-vampire/10-blood-finishing.mdx)   |

Skill spreads (choose one):

| Spread             | At 4 | At 3 | At 2 | At 1 |
| ------------------ | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Jack of all trades | —    | 1    | 8    | 10   |
| Balanced           | —    | 3    | 5    | 7    |

Academics, Craft, Performance, and Science each come with a Specialty of their own if you take any dots in them.

## Your first night

> **Storyteller:** You wake in the studio. The light under the door is gone; the city is yours again. Make your Rouse check.
>
> **Mirela's player:** A 4. Hunger goes up to 2.
>
> **Storyteller:** You can feel it — the pulse of the woman locking up the café downstairs. What do you do?
>
> **Mirela's player:** Nothing. Not the gallery staff, not the neighbours. I go to the opening tonight.
