# Attributes

_Step 4 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
Put **one** Attribute at 4, **three** at 3, **four** at 2, and **one** at 1.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Almost every roll starts with an Attribute. Your highest Attribute is what your vampire reaches for first; your lowest is where they are vulnerable. The Embrace did not change who they were: a shy person stays shy, even with fangs.

## Decide

- Is your vampire strongest in the **Physical**, **Social**, or **Mental** column?
- Which single Attribute describes them best? That one gets 4.
- What are they bad at? That one gets 1 — a weakness makes a character interesting.

| Dots | Count |
| ---- | ----- |
| 4    | 1     |
| 3    | 3     |
| 2    | 4     |
| 1    | 1     |

A short guide to each Attribute is in [Attributes and Skills](../../rules/attributes-skills.mdx).

:::note[Example: Mirela]
Decades of fine brushwork: **Dexterity 4**. She charms, notices, and knows her art history — **Charisma 3, Wits 3, Intelligence 3**. **Stamina, Manipulation, Composure,** and **Resolve** sit at 2. She was never strong: **Strength 1**.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Do vampires not get stronger than humans?</summary>

They can, but through their Blood, not their Attributes: a Blood Surge (a Rouse check) adds dice to one roll, and Disciplines such as Potence and Celerity go far beyond human limits.

</details>

:::info[Checkpoint]

- Exactly one 4, three 3s, four 2s, and one 1.
  :::
