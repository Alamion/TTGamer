# Skills and Specialties

_Step 5 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
Choose one spread — **Jack of all trades**, **Balanced**, or **Specialist** — place the dots, then add one free **Specialty** (plus automatic ones for Academics, Craft, Performance, and Science). Your predator type adds one more in step 7.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Skills are what your vampire learned, mostly in life. A roll adds a Skill to an Attribute, so a vampire with no dots in Firearms is not helpless with a gun — just unreliable.

## Decide

- Did your vampire know a little about everything, a solid amount about several things, or a lot about a few?
- Which Skills did their life before the Embrace give them?
- Which Skills do they need now to hunt and to hide? Think of how they will feed (step 7).

| Spread             | At 4 | At 3 | At 2 | At 1 |
| ------------------ | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Jack of all trades | —    | 1    | 8    | 10   |
| Balanced           | —    | 3    | 5    | 7    |
| Specialist         | 1    | 3    | 3    | 3    |

Then:

- Add **one Specialty** of your choice to a Skill you have dots in.
- If you have dots in **Academics, Craft, Performance,** or **Science**, each of them gets a Specialty too — those Skills are too broad without one.

## On your sheet

Set dots, then type the Specialty in the line next to its Skill. Separate several with commas: _Painting restoration, Gilding_.

:::note[Example: Mirela]
Mirela takes **Balanced**. At 3: **Craft, Persuasion, Academics**. At 2: **Insight, Awareness, Etiquette, Subterfuge, Investigation**. At 1: **Performance, Athletics, Stealth, Streetwise, Driving, Technology, Larceny**. Craft, Academics, and Performance bring _Painting restoration_, _Art history_, and _Singing_; her free Specialty is _Insight (Artists)_. Her Siren predator type adds _Persuasion (Seduction)_ in step 7.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Why does a restorer have Larceny?</summary>

Her studio is full of other people's locks and cases. A few Skills can come from the life she leads now rather than the job she had.

</details>

:::info[Checkpoint]

- The dot counts match your spread.
- At least one Specialty, plus one for each of Academics, Craft, Performance, Science you took.
  :::
