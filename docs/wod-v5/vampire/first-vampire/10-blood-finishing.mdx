# Blood and Finishing Touches

_Step 10 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
Agree with your group how long ago your vampires were Embraced: that sets **Generation** and **Blood Potency**. Start at **Hunger 1**. Health and Willpower size themselves from your Attributes. Add equipment and notes.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

These are the boxes you will touch most during play. Hunger rises with every Rouse check, Health and Willpower show how much more your vampire can take, and Blood Potency decides how strong your Blood is — and how picky it is about what it drinks.

## Decide

- With the Storyteller and the other players, decide how old the coterie is:

    | Embraced                        | Generation | Blood Potency | Also                                                          |
    | ------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | Recently (childer)              | 14th–16th  | 0             | Thin-bloods only                                              |
    | Recently, or decades ago        | 12th–13th  | 1             | Neonates spend 15 experience                                  |
    | A century or two ago (ancillae) | 10th–11th  | 2             | +2 points of Advantages and Flaws, −1 Humanity, 35 experience |

- Set **Hunger** to 1: every vampire wakes a little hungry.
- Check **Health** (Stamina + 3) and **Willpower** (Composure + Resolve). If an Advantage adds boxes, press **+** after the track.
- What does your vampire always carry? Add weapons under **Weapons** and everything else under **Inventory**.

## On your sheet

Write the **Generation** in the identity group (step 2). Under **Blood**, set **Hunger** and **Blood Potency**. The **Blood Potency** group below lists what your Blood Potency gives — extra dice from a Blood Surge, healing per Rouse check, your Bane severity, what feeding still works — and warns you if it is outside what your Generation allows.

:::note[Example: Mirela]
Aurelio Embraced Mirela sixteen years ago, so she is a neonate: **12th Generation**, **Blood Potency 1**, and 15 experience to spend later with the Storyteller. Stamina 2 gives her **5 Health**; Composure 2 + Resolve 2 give **4 Willpower**. She starts at **Hunger 1**.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Why would I want a low Generation?</summary>

Lower Generations are closer to the first vampires: their Blood can grow more potent. But older Kindred have more enemies, and a high Blood Potency makes animal and bagged blood useless to you.

</details>

:::info[Checkpoint]

- Generation and Blood Potency match what your group agreed, and no warning shows under Blood Potency.
- Hunger is 1; Health and Willpower show the right number of boxes.
  :::
