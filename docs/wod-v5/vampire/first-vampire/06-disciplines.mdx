# Disciplines

_Step 6 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
Take two of your clan's three **Disciplines**: one at **2** dots, the other at **1**. For each dot, pick one **power** of that Discipline at that level or lower. Caitiff pick any two Disciplines; thin-bloods have none.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Disciplines are the vampire's powers: reading minds, moving faster than sight, commanding with a look. They are what makes a scene play differently when your character is in it. Most of them cost Blood — a Rouse check — so every use brings the Beast a little closer.

## Decide

- Your clan's three Disciplines are listed in [Clans](../reference/clans.mdx). Which one fits your concept best? That one gets 2 dots.
- Which powers would your vampire use every night? Pick them first; save the dramatic ones for later.
- Each power shows how many **Rouse checks** it costs. Free powers are good for a first character.

## On your sheet

1. Under **Disciplines**, add each Discipline and set its dots.
2. Under **Powers**, add a row and choose a power. The list only offers powers of Disciplines you have, up to your dots, and fills in their level and Rouse cost.
3. Blood Sorcery and Oblivion users also get **Rituals, ceremonies, and formulas**; the block appears when you have those Disciplines.

A power that costs Blood gets a **Rouse check** button in its row. It rolls as many checks as the power costs; a high Blood Potency rerolls a failure for low-level powers (step 10).

:::note[Example: Mirela]
Toreador Disciplines are Auspex, Celerity, and Presence. Mirela takes **Auspex 2** — she always noticed what others missed — and **Presence 1**. Auspex gives her **Heightened Senses** (1) and **Premonition** (2); Presence gives her **Awe** (1). Her predator type adds a second Presence dot in step 7, and with it **Lingering Kiss** (2).
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>What is an amalgam?</summary>

A few powers need dots in a second Discipline too, written as _Dominate 3_ or similar in the power list. You can take them only when you have both.

</details>

:::info[Checkpoint]

- Two Disciplines, at 2 and at 1 dots.
- As many powers as dots, none above its Discipline's dots.
  :::
