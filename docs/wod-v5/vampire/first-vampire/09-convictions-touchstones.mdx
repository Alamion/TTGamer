# Convictions and Touchstones

_Step 9 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
Write one to three **Convictions** — rules your vampire still lives by — and give each a **Touchstone**: a living mortal who embodies it. Set **Humanity** to 7, adjusted by your predator type.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Convictions are what keep a vampire a person. When your character is about to cross a line, their Convictions should make them hesitate; breaking one, or losing a Touchstone, marks **Stains** on Humanity. Touchstones also give the Storyteller someone to put in danger — use that to make the story personal.

## Decide

- What would your vampire never do, even now? Write it as a short rule: _Never hurt a child_, _Always pay your debts_.
- Who in their life keeps that rule alive? A mortal they still see, or watch from a distance.
- Does your coterie have shared **Chronicle tenets** — lines the group agreed nobody crosses? Write them in too. Breaking a tenet also marks Stains.

## On your sheet

Each Touchstone row holds the person and their Conviction. **Humanity** starts at 7; a few predator types raise it by one (Farmer, Consensualist) or lower it (Alleycat, Blood Leech). Click the boxes to set it.

- **Dana, her old apprentice** — _Never ruin a work someone made with love._
- **Rosa, her mother in a care home** — _Never let a frightened person feel alone._
- Humanity 7: the Siren does not change it.
  :::

- One to three Convictions, each with a Touchstone.
- Humanity is 7, or 6 or 8 if your predator type says so.
