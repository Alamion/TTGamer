# What Is a Vampire?

_Step 1 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
A vampire is a person who was killed and brought back by another vampire's blood. They keep their mind and their memories, but they must drink human blood, hide from the sun and from the world, and fight the Beast inside that wants them to stop caring.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Every decision in the following steps answers one question: _who was this person, and what is left of them now?_ Knowing the premise makes the rest of the sheet feel like describing a person instead of filling in a form.

## The world

The game is set in our world, today, at night. Vampires call themselves **Kindred** and live in every big city. They have their own factions, laws, and feuds, older than any country. Most mortals never learn they exist.

## The Embrace

Your vampire was made by a **sire**, who drained them and fed them their own blood. This is the **Embrace**. Your sire's bloodline is your **clan**, which shapes your powers and your curse (step 3).

## The Masquerade

The first law of the Kindred is the **Masquerade**: never let mortals find out. A careless feeding, a power used in front of a camera, or a body left behind can bring hunters, police, or worse. Breaking it is the quickest way to make every other vampire your enemy.

## The Beast and Hunger

Inside every vampire lives the **Beast**: the predator that wants to feed, to dominate, to run from fire. **Hunger** (0 to 5) is how loud it is right now. Using your Blood makes you hungrier; feeding quiets it. The hungrier you are, the more your dice belong to the Beast.

## Humanity

**Humanity** is what is left of the person. Hurting the innocent, killing, or betraying what you believe marks your conscience. Lose enough Humanity and you become a monster that only looks like a person.

## The coterie

Vampires rarely survive alone. They gather in a **coterie**: a few Kindred who share a territory, cover for each other, and work toward something together. Your coterie is the other players' characters.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Do vampires burn in the sun?</summary>

Yes. Sunlight and fire are what they fear most. Your vampire sleeps through the day somewhere safe — their **Haven**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I have to kill people?</summary>

No. Most vampires take a little blood and leave the person alive and confused. Killing is the easy way, and it costs Humanity. Talk with your group about how dark you want the story to get, and what topics you want to keep out of it.

</details>
