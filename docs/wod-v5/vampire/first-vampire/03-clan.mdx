# Clan

_Step 3 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
Your **clan** is your sire's bloodline. It gives you three **clan Disciplines** to choose from in step 6, a **Bane** (a curse every member carries), and a **Compulsion** (what the Beast makes you do when it wins a roll).
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Other Kindred judge you by your clan before they know your name. The Bane will cause trouble every session, and the Compulsion is what you act out after a bestial failure — so pick a clan whose curse you would enjoy playing.

## Decide

- Read the one-line summaries below. Which one sounds like your sire, or like the person your vampire is becoming?
- Do you want to blend in among mortals (Toreador, Ventrue), lead a hidden life (Nosferatu, Malkavian), or fight (Brujah, Gangrel)? Any clan can do anything; these are only tendencies.
- **Caitiff** have no clan: no Bane, any two Disciplines, and the contempt of other Kindred. **Thin-bloods** are barely vampires at all; ask your Storyteller before playing one.

## On your sheet

Write the clan in the **Clan** field (step 2's group); it suggests the names as you type. The **Bane** and **Compulsion** below then show your clan's text by themselves. Some clans have a second, **variant** Bane; switch **Bane** to _Variant_ if your Storyteller allows it. If your group plays the curse differently, type over the text — the field keeps what you write.

How strong the Bane is (its **Bane severity**) depends on your Blood Potency, which you set in step 10.

:::note[Example: Mirela]
**Toreador**, like Aurelio. Her Bane: ugly surroundings make her Disciplines weaker. Her Compulsion: _Obsession_ — when the Beast wins, she becomes fixated on one beautiful thing and cannot let it go.
:::

## Common questions

No. The clan is in the blood. That is why it is worth a few minutes now.
