# Meet Mirela

:::tip[In short]
Here is the vampire we built along the way, after her first night of play. Compare her with yours, then join your coterie.
:::

## Why Mirela looks like this

- Her **Concept**, **Sire**, **Ambition**, and **Desire** came first ([step 2](./02-concept-sire.mdx)); everything else follows from a restorer who wants her sire's secret.
- **Toreador** ([step 3](./03-clan.mdx)) gives her a love of beauty that can turn into Obsession.
- **Dexterity 4** and **Strength 1** ([step 4](./04-attributes.mdx)) make her precise but fragile.
- A **Balanced** skill spread ([step 5](./05-skills-specialties.mdx)) mixes her craft with the social life she hunts in.
- **Auspex** and **Presence** ([step 6](./06-disciplines.mdx)) let her notice everything and be noticed when she wants.
- As a **Siren** ([step 7](./07-predator-type.mdx)) she feeds in intimacy, with a spurned lover as the price.
- Her **Advantages**, **Flaws** ([step 8](./08-advantages-flaws.mdx)), and **Touchstones** ([step 9](./09-convictions-touchstones.mdx)) tie her to the gallery, its patrons, and two people she would protect.

After her first night she is at **Hunger 2** (she failed the Rouse check on waking), has one **Stain** for leaving a guest of the opening too weak to stand, and spent a point of Willpower on a reroll.
