# Your First Vampire

:::tip[In short]
Ten short steps, about an hour. Each step explains one part of the character sheet, asks you a few questions, and lets you fill that part in right on the page.
:::

## What this is

A **tabletop roleplaying game** is a story you tell together. You describe what your character tries to do; the Storyteller describes the world and what happens; dice decide the moments where things could go either way. Your **character sheet** is a record of who your character is and what they are good at.

You do not need to know any rules before starting. Every step explains what a part of the sheet does _at the table_, and you will meet **Mirela Voss**, an example vampire who makes each choice alongside you.

## What you need

- About an hour (you can stop after any step — the sheet saves itself).
- A rough idea of a person you would like to play, and of the night they died. Both can change as you go.
- A vampire on the sheet. Create one now, or use the button on any step:
