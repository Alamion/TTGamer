# Predator Type

_Step 7 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
Your **predator type** is how your vampire usually feeds. It gives a **Specialty**, one more **Discipline dot**, and some **Advantages** and **Flaws**; a few also change Humanity or Blood Potency.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

Feeding is the thing your vampire does every night. The predator type tells the Storyteller where your character hunts, whom they hurt, and what goes wrong when it goes wrong. When a scene calls for feeding, its usual pool comes from here.

## Decide

- Where does your vampire find blood without getting caught? A nightclub, a hospital, their own family, the streets?
- Do they take it by force, by charm, by trickery, or by paying for it?
- Some types cost Humanity or have conditions (for example, Ventrue cannot take Bagger). Read the full text before choosing.

## On your sheet

Write the type in **Predator type** (it suggests the names). The **What the predator type gives** group then lists what to add; add each part yourself:

- the Specialty, in the Skills section (step 5);
- the Discipline dot and its power, under Disciplines (step 6);
- the Advantages and Flaws, in step 8 — mark them so you know they do not count toward your 7 and 2 points;
- a change to Humanity or Blood Potency, in steps 9 and 10.

:::note[Example: Mirela]
Mirela is a **Siren**: she feeds during real or feigned intimacy after gallery openings. The Siren gives her _Persuasion (Seduction)_, a second dot of **Presence** (she takes **Lingering Kiss**), **Looks: Beautiful** (2), and an **Enemy** (1) — a spurned lover who knows something is wrong with her.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Does my vampire only ever feed this way?</summary>

No. It is their habit, not a rule. Feeding another way is harder and may need a different pool.

</details>
