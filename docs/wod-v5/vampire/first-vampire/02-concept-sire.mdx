# Concept, Sire, Ambition, and Desire

_Step 2 of 10_

:::tip[In short]
Write a **name** and a **concept** of a few words. Name your **sire**. Then give your vampire an **Ambition** (what they want in the long run) and a **Desire** (what they want tonight).
:::

## Why it matters at the table

The concept is what the other players remember about your character. The sire is a person the Storyteller can bring into the story. Ambition and Desire tell everyone what your vampire will do when nobody tells them what to do — and the Storyteller can give you back Willpower when you chase them.

## Decide

- Who was your vampire in life? A job, a role, or a reputation is enough: _bartender who knew everyone's secrets_, _paramedic_, _failed rock star_.
- Who Embraced them, and why? A lover, a stranger, a patron who needed a servant? Did the sire stay or vanish?
- What do they want more than anything, over months or years? That is the **Ambition**.
- What do they want right now, tonight? That is the **Desire**. It changes often; rewrite it when it does.
- If your group already has a name for the chronicle, write it in **Chronicle**.

:::note[Example: Mirela]

- **Concept:** Restorer of old paintings.
- **Sire:** Aurelio, a gallery owner who never explained himself.
- **Ambition:** Restore the fresco her sire keeps hidden in the cellar.
- **Desire:** Hear someone laugh at her joke tonight.
  :::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Can my concept be what my vampire does now, not what they were?</summary>

Yes. Many players write who the vampire was in life, because it explains their Skills; some write the role they play among the Kindred. Either works.

</details>
