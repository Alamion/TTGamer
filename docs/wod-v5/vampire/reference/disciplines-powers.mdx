# Disciplines and Powers

:::tip[In short]
Each **Discipline** has powers at levels 1 to 5. You can take a power of a level up to your dots, one power per dot. A power's **Rouse checks** are its cost in Blood; an **amalgam** power also needs dots in a second Discipline.
:::

## Rituals, ceremonies, and formulas

Blood Sorcery has **rituals**, Oblivion has **ceremonies**, and Thin-Blood Alchemy has **formulas**. They take time to perform and are learned one by one, each up to the level of its Discipline; ask your Storyteller how many your character starts with. The sheet shows the **Rituals, ceremonies, and formulas** table when you have one of these Disciplines.
