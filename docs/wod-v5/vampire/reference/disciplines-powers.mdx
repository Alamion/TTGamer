# Disciplines and Powers \{#disciplines-powers}

**Disciplines** are categories of vampiric supernatural ability, such as heightened senses or unnatural strength. A Discipline’s dot rating shows how far your vampire has developed it; individual **powers** are the specific abilities learned within that category. Raising the rating opens access to higher-level powers rather than granting every power at those levels.

:::tip[In short]
Each **Discipline** has powers at levels 1 to 5. You can take a power of a level up to your dots, one power per dot. A power's **Rouse checks** are its cost in Blood; an **amalgam** power also needs dots in a second Discipline.
:::

## Powers \{#powers}

A **power** is an individual supernatural ability learned within a Discipline. Its **Discipline** identifies the category, its **level** the rating needed to learn it, and its **cost** any required Rouse checks or other payment. Knowing a Discipline does not mean knowing all its powers. Filter by level or search by name; select a power for its effect and usual dice pool. The sheet’s **Powers** table fills in the level and Rouse cost, with a **Rouse check** button for powers that cost Blood.

## Rituals, ceremonies, and formulas \{#rituals-ceremonies-and-formulas}

**Rituals**, **ceremonies**, and **formulas** are workings learned individually, with their own procedures and requirements: rituals belong to **Blood Sorcery**, ceremonies to **Oblivion**, and formulas to **Thin-Blood Alchemy**. The name records the particular working and the level its place within that Discipline; learning and performing it follows its own rules, rather than giving every working at that level. Ask your Storyteller which your character knows. The sheet shows this table when you have one of these Disciplines.
