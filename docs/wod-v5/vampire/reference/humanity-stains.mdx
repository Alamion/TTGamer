# Humanity and Stains

:::tip[In short]
**Humanity** (0–10, starting at 7) is how much of a person your vampire still is. Monstrous acts mark **Stains**. At the end of a session with Stains, make a **Remorse check**: succeed and they fade, fail and you lose a dot of Humanity.
:::

## Stains

Your Storyteller gives you Stains when your vampire acts against what they believe:

- breaking one of your **Convictions** or the coterie's **Chronicle tenets**;
- harming or losing a **Touchstone**;
- some messy criticals, and other cruel acts the Storyteller judges.

Usually one Stain, more for something terrible. Stains fill the Humanity track from its far end, toward your Humanity dots.

**On the sheet:** Humanity and its Stains share one row of ten boxes. Click a box to set Humanity; mark a Stain with a right click, Shift+Enter, or a long press. Stains never cover a Humanity dot: the sheet tells you when there is no room.

## The Remorse check

At the end of each session with at least one Stain, roll one die for every box that is neither a Humanity dot nor a Stain — at least one die. No Hunger dice here.

- **One success or more:** your vampire feels enough guilt to hold on. Clear the Stains.
- **No successes:** the Beast wins. Lose one dot of Humanity, then clear the Stains.

:::note[Example: Mirela]
Mirela ends the session with Humanity 7 and one Stain. That leaves two empty boxes: she rolls two dice.

The 8 is a success. She keeps her Humanity, and the Stain is gone.
:::

**On the sheet:** the **Remorse check** button appears while you have Stains. It rolls the right number of dice, takes a dot of Humanity on a failure, and clears the Stains either way. The message after the roll offers **Undo** if you pressed by mistake.

## When Stains overflow

If you gain more Stains than you have empty boxes, your vampire is overcome by guilt: they are **impaired** (two dice fewer on every pool) and take a point of aggravated Willpower damage for each Stain that did not fit. It lasts until the Remorse check at the end of the session — or until they give up a dot of Humanity on purpose to make peace with what they did.

## Low Humanity

The lower your Humanity, the less your vampire passes for alive: their body is colder, their face harder to read, and resting by day is longer and deeper. At Humanity 0 the Beast takes over for good, and the character is no longer yours to play.
