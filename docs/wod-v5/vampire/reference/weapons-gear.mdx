# Weapons and Gear

:::tip[In short]
A vampire’s weapons use V5 attack and damage values. A blade or firearm is carried equipment; supernatural strength and other Discipline effects follow their own rules.
:::

## Weapons \{#weapons}

A **weapon entry** records a vampire’s means of attack and its combat values: **type**, **damage bonus**, and, where relevant, **range** and **ammunition**. Type says what kind of weapon it is; damage adds to the margin of a winning attack rather than the attack pool. Range and available shots describe whether the intended attack is possible and under what circumstances.

A margin of two with a +2 weapon deals four damage before applicable protection. How the target treats it depends on their nature and the attack: vampires resist many ordinary injuries that seriously threaten mortals. The weapon entry does not replace those rules or automatically apply a Discipline’s extra effects. Use the weapon’s own rules and the Storyteller’s ruling for special ammunition, fire, or other unusual attacks.

Use the [equipment guide](../../../guide/sheet.mdx#equipment) for entering carried items and weapon cards. For marking wounds and vampire mending, see [Health](../../rules/damage-willpower.mdx#health) and [Hunger and Rouse Checks](./hunger-rouse.mdx).
