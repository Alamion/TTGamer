# Blood Potency

:::tip[In short]
**Blood Potency** (0–10) is the strength of your vampire's Blood. Higher potency gives more dice from a Blood Surge, faster healing, stronger Disciplines, and rerolls on Rouse checks — but a heavier Bane and a pickier diet. The sheet works all of it out for you.
:::

## What it changes

| As Blood Potency rises… | What happens                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Blood Surge**         | A Rouse check adds more dice to one roll: one die at first, up to five at the top.                                                    |
| **Mending**             | Each Rouse check heals more superficial damage.                                                                                       |
| **Discipline bonus**    | Discipline pools gain dice.                                                                                                           |
| **Rouse reroll**        | A failed Rouse check for a power of a low enough level is rerolled once.                                                              |
| **Bane severity**       | Your clan's Bane grows stronger.                                                                                                      |
| **Feeding**             | Animal and bagged blood slake less, then nothing; humans slake less; killing becomes the only way to bring Hunger down to its lowest. |

Blood Potency 0 means a thin-blood: no Bane, and a body almost as frail as a mortal's.

## How it grows

Blood Potency rises slowly with age, or with experience. It cannot climb above what your **Generation** allows: the 12th and 13th Generations stay at 3 or below, older Generations reach higher. Long sleep in torpor can lower it again.

## On your sheet

The **Blood Potency** group under Condition lists every effect for your current value: Blood Surge dice, mending, Discipline bonus, the highest power level a Rouse check rerolls for, Bane severity, and what feeding still works. **Blood Potency for this generation** shows the range your Generation allows, with a warning when you are outside it.

The power buttons in the **Powers** table use the reroll rule by themselves.
