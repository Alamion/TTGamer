# Glossary

:::tip[In short]
The terms you will hear at the table, with the Russian names used on this site.
:::

| English         | Русский                   | Meaning                                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kindred         | Сородичи                  | What vampires call themselves                                 |
| Embrace         | Становление               | Turning a mortal into a vampire                               |
| Sire            | Сир                       | The vampire who Embraced you                                  |
| Clan            | Клан                      | A bloodline with shared Disciplines, Bane, and Compulsion     |
| Coterie         | Котерия                   | The group of vampires played by the players                   |
| Masquerade      | Маскарад                  | The law that hides vampires from mortals                      |
| The Beast       | Зверь                     | The predator inside every vampire                             |
| Hunger          | Голод                     | 0–5; how loud the Beast is; that many dice become Hunger dice |
| Rouse check     | Проверка жажды            | One die when you use your Blood; a failure raises Hunger      |
| Blood Surge     | Прилив крови              | A Rouse check for extra dice on one roll                      |
| Messy critical  | Грязный критический успех | A critical with a 10 on a Hunger die                          |
| Bestial failure | Звериный провал           | A failed roll with a 1 on a Hunger die                        |
| Compulsion      | Побуждение                | An urge you act out after a bestial failure                   |
| Bane            | Проклятие                 | The curse every member of a clan carries                      |
| Bane severity   | Тяжесть проклятия         | How strong your Bane is; grows with Blood Potency             |
| Humanity        | Человечность              | How much of a person the vampire still is                     |
| Stain           | Пятно                     | A mark on Humanity for a monstrous act                        |
| Remorse check   | Проверка раскаяния        | End-of-session roll that clears Stains or costs Humanity      |
| Conviction      | Убеждение                 | A rule the vampire still lives by                             |
| Touchstone      | Опора                     | A living mortal who embodies a Conviction                     |
| Blood Potency   | Сила крови                | The strength of a vampire's Blood                             |
| Generation      | Поколение                 | Steps from the first vampire; limits Blood Potency            |
| Discipline      | Дисциплина                | A family of vampiric powers                                   |
| Predator type   | Тип хищника               | How a vampire usually feeds                                   |
| Haven           | Убежище                   | Where a vampire sleeps through the day                        |
| Thin-blood      | Слабокровный              | A vampire of the 14th–16th Generation, barely Kindred         |
| Caitiff         | Каитифф                   | A vampire without a clan                                      |
| Impaired        | Ослаблен                  | Two dice fewer on every pool                                  |
| Difficulty      | Сложность                 | Successes needed to succeed                                   |
