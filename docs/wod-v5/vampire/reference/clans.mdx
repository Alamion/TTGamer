# Clans \{#clans}

A **clan** is an inherited vampire lineage, passed from sire to childe through the Embrace. Most clans share an affinity for three [Disciplines](./disciplines-powers.mdx), categories of supernatural ability, a [Bane](#reading-the-bane), their continuing curse, and a [Compulsion](./hunger-rouse.mdx#bestial-failure-and-compulsions), an impulse the Beast can impose.

:::tip[In short]
A clan gives three **Disciplines** to start with, a **Bane** every member carries, and a **Compulsion** the Beast pushes you into on a bestial failure. Some clans also have a **variant Bane** your Storyteller may allow instead.
:::

Search by name or Discipline. Select a row for its summary, Bane, variant Bane, and Compulsion. On the sheet, the **Clan** field suggests these names, and the **Bane** and **Compulsion** fields show the chosen clan's texts until you write your own.

## Reading the Bane \{#reading-the-bane}

A **Bane** is a clan’s inherited supernatural curse, a continuing weakness rather than a temporary impulse. Use the clan’s usual Bane unless your Storyteller permits its listed variant instead; the [clan catalog](#clans) describes both. Its strength is **Bane severity**, derived from Blood Potency: where the Bane uses that severity, take the number from the Blood Potency group on your sheet. A [Compulsion](./hunger-rouse.mdx#bestial-failure-and-compulsions) is a separate urge imposed by the Beast. Caitiff have no clan Bane; thin-bloods have no clan at all.

The texts are short reminders in our own words, not the full rules — ask your Storyteller how a Bane plays out in your chronicle.
