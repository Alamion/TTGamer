# Clans

:::tip[In short]
A clan gives three **Disciplines** to start with, a **Bane** every member carries, and a **Compulsion** the Beast pushes you into on a bestial failure. Some clans also have a **variant Bane** your Storyteller may allow instead.
:::

Search by name or Discipline. Select a row for its summary, Bane, variant Bane, and Compulsion. On the sheet, the **Clan** field suggests these names, and the **Bane** and **Compulsion** fields show the chosen clan's texts until you write your own.

## Reading the Bane

The strength of a Bane is its **Bane severity**, which grows with Blood Potency: where a Bane says "dice equal to Bane severity", look up the number in the Blood Potency group of your sheet. Caitiff have no Bane; thin-bloods have no clan at all.

The texts are short reminders in our own words, not the full rules — ask your Storyteller how a Bane plays out in your chronicle.
