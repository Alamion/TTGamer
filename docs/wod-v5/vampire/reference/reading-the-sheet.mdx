# Reading the Sheet

:::tip[In short]
Top to bottom: who you are and how you feed, how you are holding up and how hungry you are, what you are made of and know, your Disciplines, why you hold on, who and what you have, and what you have done. Groups you rarely touch (portrait, biography, predator grants, Blood Potency, Bane) fold away.
:::

| Section              | What it holds                                                                                                                                                      | Explained in                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Vampire              | Portrait (once **Show portrait** in the biography is on); Name, Concept, Clan, Generation, Predator type, Sire, Chronicle; what the predator type gives; biography | [Step 2](../first-vampire/02-concept-sire.mdx), [Step 7](../first-vampire/07-predator-type.mdx)                                   |
| Condition            | Health and Willpower boxes; Hunger, Blood Potency, Humanity with Stains; Rouse and Remorse checks; Blood Potency effects                                           | [Hunger and Rouse checks](./hunger-rouse.mdx), [Humanity and Stains](./humanity-stains.mdx), [Blood Potency](./blood-potency.mdx) |
| Attributes           | Raw capacity, 1–5 dots                                                                                                                                             | [Attributes and Skills](../../rules/attributes-skills.mdx)                                                                        |
| Skills               | Training, 0–5 dots, with Specialties written next to the Skill                                                                                                     | [Step 5](../first-vampire/05-skills-specialties.mdx)                                                                              |
| Disciplines          | Disciplines with dots; powers with level, notes, and a Rouse check button; rituals for blood sorcerers                                                             | [Step 6](../first-vampire/06-disciplines.mdx), [Disciplines and powers](./disciplines-powers.mdx)                                 |
| Aims and convictions | Ambition, Desire, the clan's Bane and Compulsion, Touchstones, Chronicle tenets                                                                                    | [Step 3](../first-vampire/03-clan.mdx), [Step 9](../first-vampire/09-convictions-touchstones.mdx)                                 |

| Experience and Notes | Earned and spent experience; free notes                                                                                                                            | Your Storyteller                                                                                                                  |

:::info[Buttons and Undo]
The **Rouse check** and **Remorse check** buttons roll in the dice panel and change Hunger or Humanity for you. The message after the roll says what changed and offers **Undo** for a few seconds, if you pressed by mistake.
:::

:::info[Views]
The **Full sheet** view shows everything; the **Brief** view keeps only what you roll and track during play, including Hunger, Humanity, and the Rouse check. Switch with the view selector above the sheet.
:::
