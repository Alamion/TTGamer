# Weapons, Armor, and Gear

:::tip[In short]
A weapon adds its **damage bonus** to the margin of a winning attack. **Armor** turns some aggravated harm from blades and bullets into superficial. Special **gear** mostly adds dice to one kind of test.
:::

## Weapons \{#weapons}

A **weapon entry** describes something a hunter can attack with and its V5 combat values. **Type** identifies the weapon category; its **damage bonus** adds to the margin of a successful attack, not to the attack pool. **Range** describes where it can be used effectively, and **ammunition**, where recorded, tracks available shots. These details help the Storyteller judge the attack’s circumstances. Hunter uses broad weapon types rather than separate rules for every brand or calibre; choosing a type on the sheet fills its damage bonus.

## Armor

Each point of armor turns one point of aggravated damage from a blade or a bullet into superficial, which is then halved as usual. Most monsters already shrug off such wounds, so armor mainly helps humans.

## Gear

A few tools that help against the unnatural or around it. The **Inventory** cards on the sheet suggest these names and fill in the effect.
