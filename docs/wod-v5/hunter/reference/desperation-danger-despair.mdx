# Desperation, Danger, and Despair

:::tip[In short]
**Desperation** lets hunters add dice when the hunt is on the line. A **1** on those dice costs something: the cell's **Danger** rises, or the hunter falls into **Despair**.
:::

## Desperation — how far the cell will go

Desperation is a rating from 0 to 5 shared by the whole cell. It climbs when hunts go badly and settles when the cell wins.

When a roll directly pursues the hunt — following the Creed, chasing the quarry, protecting the innocent from it — the player may add dice equal to the cell's Desperation. Roll them together with the normal pool, but keep them apart (a different colour helps). Their successes count like any other. On the site, add them with the special-die button in the dice panel's **WoD** tab (V5 mode, Desperation line): they are drawn in their own colour, and the roll says when one of them shows a 1.

## The price of a 1

If any Desperation die shows a **1**, the hunter must pay. The player chooses:

- **Push through:** the action goes ahead, and the cell's **Danger** rises by one.
- **Break:** the hunter falls into **Despair**.

## Danger — how much the monsters know

Danger is also a shared rating. It measures how exposed the cell is. As it rises, the Storyteller brings harder opposition: the quarry prepares, allies turn cautious, other things start watching. Careful, quiet hunts keep it low.

## Despair — when the Drive breaks

A hunter in Despair has lost faith in their own reasons for hunting. While in Despair, they cannot add Desperation dice, and their Drive works against them instead of pushing them forward. To come back, the hunter has to act on their **Redemption** — the line you wrote in step 3.

## Keeping sheets in sync

Cells do not have their own documents yet, so each hunter's sheet carries a copy of Desperation and Danger. When the numbers change, everyone updates their sheet.
