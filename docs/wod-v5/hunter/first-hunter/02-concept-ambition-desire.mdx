# Concept, Ambition, and Desire

_Step 2 of 9_

:::tip[In short]
Write a short **Concept** (who they are), a long-term **Ambition**, and a small, immediate **Desire**.
:::

## Why it matters at the table

When you are unsure what your hunter would do, these three lines answer it. The Storyteller also uses them to build scenes your hunter cares about.

## Decide

- What does your hunter do all day when they are not hunting? That is a good start for a **Concept** — _night-shift paramedic_, _retired boxer_, _true-crime podcaster_.
- If the story went perfectly, what would your hunter finally achieve? That is their **Ambition**.
- What do they want right now, this week, that has nothing to do with saving the world? That is their **Desire**.
- The **Redemption** line can wait until step 3.
