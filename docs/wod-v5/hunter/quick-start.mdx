# Quickstart

:::tip[In short]
Roll **Attribute + Skill** in ten-sided dice, count **6+**, pairs of 10s count four. Mark harm as **superficial** or **aggravated**. When the hunt matters, add **Desperation dice** — and pay if one shows a 1.
:::

## What you need

- A hunter sheet open in the [Sheet Manager](/universal_sheet) (a second tab works well).
- A handful of ten-sided dice, or the site's dice roller.
- Two to five players and a Storyteller.

## The one roll you need to know

1. The Storyteller names an **Attribute** and a **Skill**. Add their dots: that is your pool.
2. Roll the pool. Each die showing **6 or more** is a success; **each pair of 10s** is worth four.
3. Reach the **Difficulty** (usually 2–4 successes) and you succeed. Extra successes make the result better.
4. Once per roll, spend a point of **Willpower** to reroll up to three failed dice.

:::note[Example: Lena]
Lena checks a body that is far too cold for how recently it died: **Intelligence 2 + Medicine 3**, Difficulty 2, and her _Trauma_ specialty adds a die.

## Getting hurt and pushing through

- **Health** has Stamina + 3 boxes; **Willpower** has Composure + Resolve boxes.
- **Superficial** harm fades quickly; **aggravated** harm stays. Aggravated marks fill boxes first.
- No empty boxes left? New superficial harm turns an old superficial mark aggravated. A full track means you are **impaired**.
- On the sheet, click a box to mark it: once for superficial (╱), twice for aggravated (×), a third time to clear it.

Details: [Damage and Willpower](../rules/damage-willpower.mdx).

## The hunter twist

- **Desperation** (0–5) belongs to the whole cell. When a roll directly serves the hunt, add that many **Desperation dice**. Their successes count normally.
- If any Desperation die shows a **1**, the hunter pays: either the cell's **Danger** rises (the monsters notice), or the hunter falls into **Despair** — their Drive stops helping until they find their way back.
- **Danger** measures how exposed the cell is. The higher it climbs, the harder the night gets.

Details: [Desperation, Danger, and Despair](./reference/desperation-danger-despair.mdx).

## Your first scene

> **Storyteller:** The night nurse swears the man in bed 4 had no pulse an hour ago. Now the bed is empty and the window is open. What do you do?
>
> **Lena's player:** I check the window frame for blood. Wits and Awareness?
>
> **Storyteller:** Wits plus Awareness, Difficulty 2.
>
> **Lena's player:** Six dice… three successes.
>
> **Storyteller:** No blood — but frost on the inside of the glass, in the shape of a hand.
