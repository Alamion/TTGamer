# Dark Pack notice

:::tip[In short]
TTGamer is a free fan project. World of Darkness 5th Edition names and game terms appear here under Paradox Interactive's Dark Pack policy. This page is the one place with the full notice; sheets show only the small Dark Pack badge that links here.
:::

## What this covers

The Dark Pack is Paradox Interactive's community policy for fan-made World of Darkness material. It lets fans build tools and write about the games as long as the work stays free, is marked as unofficial, and carries the required notice and badge.

On this site that applies to:

- the **World of Darkness 5th Edition** documentation, including Hunter: the Reckoning;
- **hunter and V5 mortal character sheets** and every page template made for them, shipped or your own;
- **your own settings and document types built on the V5 rules**;
- **exported V5 files and templates**, which carry the notice text inside the file.

The policy covers World of Darkness 5th Edition material only. The classic World of Darkness 2nd Edition rules and the Star Wars conversion built on them are outside it, so their sheets carry no badge.

## What it means for you

- Everything here is free. Nothing is sold, and no World of Darkness content sits behind a purchase.
- This is not an official product. For the real rules, get the books from the publisher.
- Rules pages explain mechanics in our own words; they are not copies of the books.

The badge in the corner of a hunter sheet is a pointer to this page. You don't need to do anything with it.
