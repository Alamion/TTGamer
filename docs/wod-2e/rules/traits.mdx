# Attributes and Abilities

Traits are named ratings that describe a character's capabilities. Attributes cover basic capacity; Abilities cover what the character can do through instinct, practice, or study.

## Attributes \{#attributes}

Attributes rate a character's basic physical, social, and mental capabilities. **Physical** contains Strength, Dexterity, and Stamina; **Social** contains Charisma, Manipulation, and Appearance; **Mental** contains Perception, Intelligence, and Wits. The generic sheet uses one to five dots for each. More dots mean greater capacity, rather than guaranteed success.

An Attribute often combines with an Ability to form a pool of ten-sided dice: Dexterity 3 plus Drive 2 gives five dice for a difficult driving manoeuvre. The Storyteller chooses the traits and difficulty appropriate to the action. Abilities represent experience in a particular activity, rather than the underlying capacity rated by an Attribute.

## Appearance \{#appearance}

**Appearance** is the Social Attribute for physical attractiveness and the impression made by a character's looks. It can matter when an immediate visual impression is decisive, such as catching a guest's attention at a reception. Its dot rating is separate from the Appearance description card's written details, such as height, hair, or distinguishing features. A description does not determine the rating automatically.

## Abilities \{#abilities}

Abilities rate particular capabilities, divided into **Talents**, **Skills**, and **Knowledges**. Talents are intuitive, Skills are practical learned techniques, and Knowledges rely on study and information. The full sheet and brief collect all three categories under **Abilities**. A zero means no rated experience; one to five dots show increasing competence.

Custom entries let the group name an additional Ability in the appropriate category. A specialization records a narrower focus, such as night driving within Drive; it is not a second Ability rating. At four or more dots in an Attribute or Ability, an applicable specialty lets you reroll each 10 for an additional success while keeping its original success. Select **S** only when that specialty applies to the action; below four dots it has no mechanical effect. Classic rolls always cancel successes with ones; the Star Wars conversion's Experienced and Practiced benefits do not apply. Existing flags remain stored for compatibility even when their controls are hidden. See the [rating guide](../../guide/template-editor/elements.mdx#rating) for editing dots and specialization controls.

## Talents \{#talents}

Talents are Abilities based on instinct and experience rather than formal technique. Alertness, Athletics, and Empathy are examples on this sheet. With no dots in a Talent, roll the relevant Attribute alone at the usual difficulty unless a particular rule says otherwise; being untrained adds no general Talent penalty.

## Expression \{#expression}

**Expression** is the Talent for conveying ideas and feelings effectively through words or other communication. A speaker explaining an evacuation plan to a frightened crowd might use it to make the message clear and compelling. Performance instead covers practised artistic presentation, while traits such as Intimidation or Subterfuge address threats or deception. Choose the trait for what the character is trying to achieve.

## Skills \{#skills}

Skills are the category of Abilities learned through practical training, such as Drive, Melee, or Stealth. The category sits within **Abilities**, alongside Talents and Knowledges. Attempting a Skill with no dots normally uses the relevant Attribute at a difficulty increased by one, if the task is possible without training. The Storyteller decides whether specialised equipment or instruction is also necessary.

## Animal Ken \{#animal-ken}

**Animal Ken** is the Skill for understanding animal behaviour and handling or training animals. It might help someone calm a frightened dog blocking a doorway or recognise that a horse is about to bolt. It does not grant supernatural communication or control, and an animal's limits and temperament still matter.

## Drive \{#drive}

**Drive** is the Skill for operating ground vehicles, especially when conditions demand more than routine driving. Keeping a van steady on an icy bend during a chase could call for it. The name concerns vehicle handling, rather than a character's motivation; it does not automatically cover aircraft, boats, or every unfamiliar machine. Agree specialised vehicle use with the Storyteller.

## Melee \{#melee}

**Melee** is the Skill for fighting with hand-held close-combat weapons. Striking with a baton or defending with a sword can use it. Unarmed punches, grapples, and kicks use Brawl instead. The weapon and the chosen combat rules determine damage; the Skill rating contributes to the relevant action's pool.

## Security \{#security}

**Security** is the Skill for working with locks, alarms, and physical security systems, including bypassing their protection. The Russian sheet calls it **Взлом**, emphasising that practical use. A character might use it to pick a storeroom lock or disable a door alarm. It is not a general rating for being safe; computer intrusion may require Computer instead, depending on the system and task.

## Knowledges \{#knowledges}

Knowledges are Abilities based on study, specialised information, and learned understanding. Medicine, Law, and Occult are examples on this sheet. With no dots in a Knowledge, a character normally cannot attempt a task that requires that knowledge; an Attribute alone does not replace missing expertise. The Storyteller may allow common facts that anyone in the setting could know without a roll.

## Occult \{#occult}

**Occult** is knowledge of supernatural traditions, beliefs, and reported phenomena. Comparing an unfamiliar symbol with accounts of a local cult could use it during an investigation. Knowing a legend does not prove that it is true, and knowing about a supernatural power does not give the character that power. The Storyteller determines what reliable information is available.
