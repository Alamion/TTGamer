# Weapons and Armor

Equipment entries record what a character carries or wears and the statistics the group needs during play. Their effects come from the chosen game's equipment and combat rules.

## Weapons \{#weapons}

Weapon entries are records of carried or used weapons and their game statistics. **Damage** stores the weapon's damage value or expression; **Range** records its reach or firing distance. **Ammo** is the ammunition currently loaded and **Capacity** is its maximum, so 4 / 6 records four rounds in a weapon that holds six. These entries can include descriptive text and do not resolve an attack or roll damage automatically.

Apply the agreed weapon rules, resolve the action, and update ammunition after firing or reloading. An ordinary sheet roll does not spend ammunition. See the [equipment guide](../../guide/sheet.mdx#equipment) for adding entries and editing their values.

## Armor \{#armor}

Armor entries record protective clothing or equipment and the values needed to use it. **Class** is a descriptive classification of the armor, rather than a universal target number. **AR** means armor rating: the number of dice added to a soak roll. **Dex pen** is the restriction on movement: increase the difficulty of rolls involving Dexterity by that amount. It does not subtract dots from Dexterity or dice from the pool.

The generic sheet stores these fields as editable text. It does not automatically add AR to a soak pool, change a roll's difficulty, or resolve damage. Apply the values manually according to the group's rules; several armor entries do not establish that their protection stacks. See the [equipment guide](../../guide/sheet.mdx#equipment) for the controls.
