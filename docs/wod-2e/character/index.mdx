# Character

A character is the person a player portrays in the shared story. Descriptions say who that person is; ratings and resources describe what they can do and how they are holding up.

## Identity \{#identity}

Identity is the collection of details that identifies and describes a character. **Name** belongs to the fictional person; **Player** names the real person portraying them. Concept gives the premise, Nature the inner motive, Demeanor the outward personality, and Chronicle the shared story's title. Appearance details and Biography are descriptions, rather than ratings or bonuses.

See [Concept](#concept), [Nature](#nature), [Demeanor](#demeanor), and [Chronicle](#chronicle) for those fields, and [Appearance](../rules/traits.mdx#appearance) for the separate rated Attribute.

## Concept \{#concept}

Concept is a short premise for the character: who they are and what makes them worth following. For example, _a night-shift nurse searching for a missing neighbour_ suggests both useful experience and a reason to enter danger. Write a phrase that can guide later choices of traits and relationships; the phrase itself grants no dice or points.

## Nature \{#nature}

Nature is the personality archetype that describes a character's deepest motives. It concerns what satisfies them internally, even when their public behaviour suggests otherwise. Choose a short archetype name and agree with the Storyteller what would fulfil it. Meaningful actions that fulfil Nature can restore spent [Willpower](../rules/condition.mdx#willpower) when the Storyteller awards recovery; writing the name alone restores nothing.

## Chronicle \{#chronicle}

A Chronicle is the ongoing shared story made up of individual game sessions. Write its title here, such as _The Last Night Bus_. Characters taking part in that story can use the same title. This field names the campaign, while Biography describes one character's past.

## Demeanor \{#demeanor}

Demeanor is the personality a character presents to other people. Write an archetype or short description of their usual outward behaviour. A character whose Nature is Caregiver might adopt a stern, commanding Demeanor to keep frightened neighbours safe: the protective motive and the public manner describe different things, and need not match.

## Condition \{#condition}

Condition is the full sheet's area for injuries, Willpower, Initiative, and Virtues. Health marks and available Willpower change during play; the Willpower rating and Virtue ratings describe more lasting capacities. Initiative is calculated from other traits. Follow [Health](../rules/condition.mdx#health), [Willpower](../rules/condition.mdx#willpower), [Initiative](../rules/condition.mdx#initiative), or [Virtues](../rules/condition.mdx#virtues) for the individual rules.

## Resolve \{#resolve}

Resolve is the brief sheet's heading for Virtues, Willpower, and Initiative. These are the same values shown under [Condition](#condition) on the full sheet; the brief puts Health in its own block. Resolve has no rating, pool, or roll of its own, and is not an additional Attribute.
