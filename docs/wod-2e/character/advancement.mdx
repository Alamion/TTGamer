# Experience

## Experience \{#experience}

Experience points (XP) record the advancement a character earns through play and can spend on improvements. The Storyteller awards points for the group's progress and the character's experiences; the chosen rules determine the price and requirements of an improvement. Agree the purchase, update the relevant trait, and record its cost.

**Total XP** is everything earned so far, including points already used. **Spent** is the cumulative amount paid for improvements. **Available** is calculated as Total XP minus Spent: with 12 total and 7 spent, 5 remain. Earning 3 more raises Total XP to 15; buying an improvement for 4 raises Spent to 11. Recording expenditure does not raise traits, and raising a trait does not deduct XP automatically. Keep Spent at or below Total XP.
