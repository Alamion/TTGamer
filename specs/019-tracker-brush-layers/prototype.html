Decisions in this prototype

  • Brush. Only with the legend on (so never on one line). One brush per tracker at a time; a click on the active item turns it off. The brush lives while the sheet is open and is not saved. A read-only sheet shows the legend as plain labels.
  • Brush click. The box gets the brush mark on the brush's layer; if that layer already holds that mark, it is cleared. The other layer never changes. No cycling while a brush is on.
  • No brush. A click cycles the fill marks only (empty → first → … → last → empty) and keeps the outline. A tracker with no fill marks cycles its outline marks instead.
  • Layers. Each mark is a Fill (background and symbol, as today) or an Outline (a thick frame in the mark's color; its symbol shows only when the box has no fill). A box holds at most one fill and one outline.
  • Reading. The total row and "out" read the fill layer only. Shortening a tracker folds each layer separately; hidden-value counts include outlines.
  • Built-in trackers stay fill-only (the game stores one mark per box); the layer setting is locked there, the brush works.
  • Stored values of trackers saved before this change are fill marks; nothing is converted.
  • Force Points here are an own tracker. Turning the Star Wars Force Points resources into a built-in tracker is a later task.

Prototype only: nothing is kept. Lengths, text columns, and the Source switch work as built in spec 018 and are left out here.
