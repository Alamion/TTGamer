Decisions in this prototype

  • Fill from the start. Marking box N on a layer marks boxes 1…N and clears the rest of that layer; clicking the last marked box takes off just that one. Each layer runs on its own; copies too.
  • Left click puts the first fill mark, right click the first outline mark, by the same rules (a run from the start, or the cycle on trackers without it; this also applies to every tracker). Other marks come from the legend brush.
  • Fills stay inside the outline (own trackers that fill from the start, off by default): a fill run stops at the last framed box.
  • Minimum on current. Star Wars Willpower cannot go below Passion + Self-Control: those filled boxes are darker and stay; current Willpower may raise its maximum, as the dot row does today.
  • Count reads filled boxes, and "filled / framed" once any box is framed. Deepest level stays the default.
  • Built-in pool. A pool resource gets a third look, Tracker, next to dots and numbers: boxes up to the game's limit (10), the maximum framed, the current points filled. Current never passes the maximum; framed boxes under the maximum's minimum (Self-Control) are darker and stay.
  • Same data. The values stay the character's own: the dots page below the tracker shows them, and changes on either side show on the other.
  • Shipped pages. The Star Wars full sheet replaces "Max Force Points" + "Force Points" with this one tracker; the brief sheet keeps its numbers.

Prototype only: nothing is kept. Columns, copies, lengths, and the Source switch work as built in specs 018–019.
