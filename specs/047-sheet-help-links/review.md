# Sheet help review

Read each document’s Full sheet before its Brief, then the shared Element types. The table records
the approved decisions, amended by the maintainer’s round 2 review. Each row preserves its draft id
(`template/node` or `type:control`); repeated ids from different drafts are merged. `where` and
`what a newcomer sees` identify the visible place; the next columns compare its own link today with
the proposed link, quote the current English opening, assess the target, and explain the decision.
Empty draft marks are retained as historical metadata; the amendments below govern implementation.

Rules applied from [the specification](spec.md):

- Sections, groups and unfamiliar terms get help where a newcomer needs it (FR-001, FR-009). `none`
  records an expected but deliberately suppressed mark: a broader container, a term already
  explained by its card, or plain Notes that might be mistaken for Markdown. Obvious portraits,
  appearance/biography prompts and other ordinary descriptions without existing links are omitted.
- The first paragraph must explain the labelled thing; new or rewritten rules must be original
  paraphrases starting with a plain definition (FR-002, FR-007). A section and its contained group
  or field cannot share a target; this also applies to any linked ancestor. A linked container’s
  sole content has no separate explicit link, including a broader destination (FR-003, FR-010).
- Rules stay in the document’s system: Star Wars, WoD 2e, or V5 (shared V5 mechanics may serve its
  Mortal, Hunter, Vampire and Storyteller pages). Interface help goes to `/docs/guide/` (FR-005).
- Automatic element help applies only to Markdown and mentions on full pages, including user pages.
  Other control explanations remain in the template editor. Explicit links take precedence;
  `hideHelp` suppresses only the default. Briefs receive no defaults. `via type` means a default,
  not a stored template link. Round 2 found no redundant stored links at this HEAD; removing the
  other automatic defaults removes the second marks inside linked cards across all systems.
- Every destination needs the same explicit anchor in EN and RU before use (FR-006). A section’s
  mark is a keyboard-reachable link named `Documentation for <section>`, outside its fold button;
  opening help must not fold the section (FR-008). The approved destinations and negative decisions
  are enforced by tests.

Target status is shared by all rows using that destination: **exists** means the route and target
are present in both languages and the opening fits; **definition missing** means the EN target is
present but its opening needs repair (possibly also anchor pairing); **write** means a page or
fragment is missing in at least one language. For an EN-only target with adequate prose, `write`
means **anchor pairing only**, listed separately below. `—` means no target. No unwritten sentence
is invented: quotes strip source formatting; `— (not written)` means the exact destination is
absent; `— (no opening paragraph)` means a heading immediately contains subheadings. Tutorial “Step
N of N” quotes are opening markers, not definitions, even when useful definitions follow.

Evidence: the four system drafts and `context/047/inventory/{templates,docs}.md`, checked against
current template declarations, canonical labels, and EN/RU documentation sources. A read-only pass
using the inventory’s Docusaurus slug/heading parser confirmed all 117 inventoried routes, opening
quotes and bilingual anchor states. The inventory declares **23 pages: 12 full and 11 briefs**,
which supersedes the estimate in the spec introduction. Russian existence and anchor pairing were
checked; translation quality and rendered placement still need the later manual walk.

- **Droid rules and sheet state disagree.** Droid docs permit Force Point use and Dark Side
  Resistance loss, while the shipped droid pages omit both. Their Species label also leaves the
  intended model/identity entry unclear. Resolve that contract before finalizing the droid help.

  do not change with later Virtue increases, while controls apply derived minimums. Willpower is
  described as static in Star Wars; shared resource instructions must not imply it is spendable.

  inventory binding beside Implants & Cyberware; their division is unclear. Memory examples use
  Firearms, Computer and Occult rather than the shipped Star Wars vocabulary.
- **Group health has conflicting models.** Encounter guidance describes a shared health pool while
  the shipped fodder group tracks members separately; creature guidance also disagrees with the
  3/5/7 selector. Establish the intended model before writing the Members passage.
- **Force-derived values need rules/formula reconciliation.** Saber initiative and running/jumping
  are displayed as calculated values or multipliers; the new definition must identify their actual
  baselines and units rather than infer them from the labels.
- **WoD 2e inheritance needs a rules decision.** The engine inherits Star Wars-style trait flags,

  without its own rules docs. Establish what the engine intends before describing generic WoD 2e
  mechanics. The proposed WoD 2e namespace has no existing EN or RU pages.
- **Some group names conceal different meanings.** Skills is both an umbrella and one subgroup;
  Resolve groups Virtues, Willpower and Initiative; Appearance is both prose and a numerical trait.
  Review the naming and grouping together with its translations, rather than adding more marks.
- **System slots and Configuration are independent records.** Clarify their intended relationship
  and the rules for using each; do not imply fixed physical compartments or automatic syncing.
- **Mortal moral traits need a scope decision.** The V5 Mortal page has Touchstones, Convictions and
  Tenets, but the shared docs do not establish their mortal effects. Its new help must not silently
  apply Vampire-specific consequences. Review Russian Conviction terminology at the same time.
- **Creed Fields has no established meaning.** Neither the source nor the tutorial explains what it
  stores. The Creed and Drive card actually contains Redemption and Creed Fields; settle the card’s
  contents and terminology before inventing a definition. Rows using its new fragment stay `write`
  pending that decision.
- **Action help is not specified by D4.** Rouse and Remorse buttons, including per-power Rouse,
  change state, but the field/primitive help design does not cover buttons. Decide accessible help
  placement for these actions; the review does not silently widen the mechanism.
- **Help placement for hidden labels needs rendered validation.** Identity headings hide their
  existing marks. Check that retained help has a visible, accessible location; General note’s
  Markdown default is deliberately suppressed. Brief abbreviations and raw English fallback labels
  also need a rendered bilingual check before being classified as defects.
- **Rules help can mask interaction help.** An explicit field link takes precedence over its type
  default. Rules targets on trackers and resource fields need onward guide links. Humanity also
  hides its mark legend: the guide must explain Stains from the opposite end and alternate input.
- **Shared V5 prose has line-specific limits.** Reconcile rest-based Health recovery with Vampire
  mending, and the Hunter synchronization text with existing Group documents. Membership alone does
  not synchronize Desperation or Danger.
